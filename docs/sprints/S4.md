### Continued engineering and acceptance status

Status: **S4 remains incomplete; S5–S7 have not started.** No human chess review
has been supplied. The [human review worksheet](S4-chess-review.md) distinguishes

Changes in the continuation:

- Versioned `move_review` JSON is persisted atomically beside each ply's engine
  evidence. API snapshots use that persisted review and do not replay every PV
  on each poll. Conflicting evidence/review retries are rejected.
- Migration `0003` adds nullable persisted reviews and a nonnegative durable
  verification ledger. Search reservations commit before engine work, remain
  spent on timeout/crash, and are fenced by the job attempt token. Budget comes
  from completed base searches plus the current bounded base comparison; total
  reservations cannot exceed 15% of that work.
- Additional searches receive the preset's unchanged budget. A reservation also
  includes 100ms scheduling headroom and the adapter's one-second cancellation
  allowance. Handshake and search cleanup now use bounded waits; unresponsive
  engines are killed/reaped and fail as engine failures, without emitting proof.
- Great rechecks the closest runner-up at the same root and preset budget.
  Brilliant checks the unrestricted strongest reply, six subsequent plies and
  expected score, then separately checks a safe pre-move alternative retaining
  the offered piece. It excludes forced moves, recaptures and previously attacked
  pieces; absent survival evidence is uncertain. Failed Brilliant verification
  may fall back to a separately reserved Great check.
- Miss requires a verified legal forced mate or a narrowly proved material
  continuation. A cooperative single PV is insufficient: material proof requires
  forced opponent replies and an inspected opponent response after the gain.
  Other material motifs remain unsupported. Optional invalid output omits a badge;
  cancellation, deadline and process failures preserve their lifecycle behavior.
- Proof IDs, root positions, legal engine lines, search reservations and policy
  version are persisted with badges. Supported themes derive only from proofs.
  The frontend validates proof identity, evidence/score consistency, Great
  uniqueness and runner-up, summary counts and themes, and displays the checked
  lines. Brilliance is explicitly a heuristic; Quick remains provisional.
- New cache identities use feature version `s4-v1`. Existing identical-engine
  `none-v1` / `pending-v1` jobs can finish under their original identity; caches
  do not silently cross the feature-version change.

Review dispositions (equivalent Git `/diff` and independent Standards/Spec
`/review` from the original fixed sprint baseline, including untracked files):
